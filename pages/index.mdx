## What is VFX Ark?
An open-sourced VFX library containing multiple and unique effects to aid in making VFX for the 
[Roblox](https://Roblox.com) platform.

## Why VFX Ark?
VFX Ark combines the quality of built-in features, the freedom of customization, optimization,
and variation of effects.

The library's effects were designed to be a middle ground for both VFX artists who don't have
knowledge in coding or math and for those who do.

**No coding knowledge?** VFX Ark gives a lot of built-in customization with some effects having 
40+ unique properties.

**Coding knowledge?** VFX Ark allows for custom functions for 90% of its algorithms and effect 
equations, the library is fully open-sourced, so feel free to edit it to your heart's content.
